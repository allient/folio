# Reader emulator captures


The Knotes captures show the revised PDF reader, its reader tools sheet, and the EPUB reader. They were captured from the Android emulator after installing the debug build. PDF left/right page turns and EPUB paged-mode left/right swipes were manually exercised; the EPUB continuous-scroll preference remains vertical.

The study view capture (`knotes-study-view.png`) shows the current PDF on one pane and a newly created, editable Knotes notebook on the other. Its first line records the source title and page; when a passage was selected before opening Study, that quote is added below it. On narrow screens the two panes stack vertically; on wide tablets they sit side by side.

Inspiration: the split-view idea of e-ink reader apps (horizontal or vertical split for reading next to handwriting or typing). Screenshots of other apps are kept out of the repository.
