Folio 1.0.9
- Reader: all your saved pens (colours, several highlighters) are one tap away in the top bar, with an eye button for reading mode. Tap the selected pen for its settings.
- New option: hide the bar while writing (Gestures).
- Turn pages with two fingers while a pen is active.
- Drawings now appear together with the page, and the bar no longer blinks.
- Safer with lots of handwriting: less memory and no crash if memory runs out.
