Folio 1.0.5
- Swipe to turn notebook pages with a smooth slide; swipe past the last page to add one.
- Select and delete several pages at once. Import a PDF or EPUB as a notebook.
- Reader: the notebook's pens, a movable selection bar with word selection (also with the stylus), more highlight colors and a color picker.
- Smoother writing: no flicker when you lift the pen.
- A new About screen with links to the website and GitHub.
- New icons throughout.
